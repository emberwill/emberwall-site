Off Script

You will meet strangers, one at a time, in ordinary places: a ferry crossing a harbour, a bus, a waiting room, a gate at an airport. There is nothing to collect and no score.

Type what you do, or what you say. Words in quotation marks are spoken exactly as you type them; start with ? to ask the narrator about the scene. You can ask, answer, refuse, apologize, hold your ground, wait, or leave.

Some people will meet you as an equal. Some won't. Some can be invited to. Getting it wrong here costs nothing: after each meeting you can see what actually passed between you, and each new stranger is chosen to test what the earlier meetings revealed.
